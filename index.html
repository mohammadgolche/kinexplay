KinexPlay: AR Motion Mobile Game

Step into the future of mobile gaming with KinexPlay — an AI-powered AR motion game where your body is the controller. No VR headsets, no wearables, no controllers. Just you, your moves, and non-stop action.

How it works: Your phone’s front camera tracks your full-body motion in real time. Punch like in a boxing game, dodge incoming obstacles, and react fast to dynamic challenges. It’s a mobile experience inspired by Kinect-style gameplay — full-body motion control, now right on your phone. Every move feels like stepping into an action-packed martial arts fighting and sports game.
— just launch and move!

    🔥 Key Features:

  • AI-powered motion tracking
  • AR gameplay that feels immersive and fun
  • Real-time body action — punch, dodge, fight, smash
  • Fast, challenging, and addictive gameplay
  • Easy to start, hard to master
  • Inspired by boxing games, martial arts, and classic fighting games

    🎮 Why you’ll love it:

  • A one-of-a-kind AR motion game on mobile
  • Fun for gamers, families, and anyone who loves sports and action
  • Perfect for fans of boxing games, martial arts challenges, fighting games, and motion-based gameplay
  • Kinect-style gaming on your phone — no extra gear needed
  • Short sessions or long play — always engaging
