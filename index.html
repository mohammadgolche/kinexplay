KinexPlay: AR Motion Mobile Game

Step into the future of mobile gaming with KinexPlay — an AI-powered AR motion game where your body is the controller. No VR headsets, no wearables, no controllers. Just you, your moves, and non-stop action.

How it works: Your phone’s front camera tracks your full-body motion in real time. Punch like in a boxing game, dodge incoming obstacles, and react fast to dynamic challenges. It’s a mobile experience inspired by Kinect-style gameplay — full-body motion control, now right on your phone. Every move feels like stepping into an action-packed martial arts fighting and sports game.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

KinexPlay respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be processed when you use the KinexPlay mobile game


1. Information Collection

KinexPlay does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information through a registration system.

The game uses your device's camera solely for real-time body-tracking gameplay features. Camera frames are processed locally on your device in real time and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted to any external servers.


2. In-App Purchases

KinexPlay offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock the full game. Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. KinexPlay does not directly collect or store your payment card details.

KinexPlay uses RevenueCat to manage purchase information and verify purchase entitlements. RevenueCat may process technical and transaction-related information as necessary to provide this service.


3. Third-Party Services

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may process information according to their respective privacy policies and the services they provide.


4. Data Security and Retention

KinexPlay does not operate a separate account database for players. Information processed by third-party services is subject to their applicable data handling and retention practices.


5. Children's Privacy

KinexPlay does not collect, store, or share personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If you have any questions or concerns about privacy, please contact us.


6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.


By using this game, you agree to this privacy policy.
